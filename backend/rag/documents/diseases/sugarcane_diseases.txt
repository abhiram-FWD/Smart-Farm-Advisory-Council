
SUGARCANE CROP - DISEASE KNOWLEDGE

1. RED ROT

Causal organism:
Colletotrichum falcatum (fungus).

Possible symptoms:
- Yellowing and drying of leaves.
- Internal red discolouration of stalk tissues.
- White patches may appear within the red internal tissue.
- Affected stalks may develop an unpleasant smell.
- Severe infection may cause stalk deterioration.

Monitoring:
Inspect affected stalks by examining internal tissue.
Record the distribution of affected clumps.
Seek expert confirmation when symptoms are uncertain.

2. SMUT

Causal organism:
Sporisorium scitamineum (fungus).

Possible symptoms:
- A long, black, whip-like structure emerges
  from the growing point.
- Thin, grassy-looking shoots may develop.
- Infected plants may show reduced growth.
- Cane development and yield may be affected.

Monitoring:
Inspect growing points for characteristic whip structures.
Record affected clumps and their locations.

3. SUGARCANE GRASSY SHOOT DISEASE

Causal agent:
Phytoplasma.

Possible symptoms:
- Excessive production of thin shoots.
- Leaves become narrow and pale.
- Plants develop a grassy appearance.
- Stunted growth and reduced cane development.

Monitoring:
Inspect plants for abnormal shoot multiplication.
Compare affected plants with healthy plants nearby.
Seek diagnostic confirmation when required.

4. SUGARCANE MOSAIC

Causal agent:
Sugarcane mosaic virus and related viruses.

Possible symptoms:
- Light and dark green mosaic patterns on leaves.
- Irregular leaf colour patterns.
- Reduced plant vigour.
- Growth may become stunted in affected plants.

Monitoring:
Inspect leaves for characteristic mosaic patterns.
Record affected plants and their distribution.
Check for possible insect vectors.

5. PINEAPPLE DISEASE

Causal organism:
Ceratocystis paradoxa (fungus).

Possible symptoms:
- Poor germination of planted cane setts.
- Internal tissue discolouration.
- Softening and deterioration of affected setts.
- A pineapple-like smell may develop in infected material.

Monitoring:
Inspect planting material and affected setts.
Record germination failures and visible internal damage.

6. GENERAL DISEASE ADVISORY

Sugarcane diseases can produce overlapping symptoms.

Consider:
- Crop age and variety.
- Planting material health.
- Soil and drainage conditions.
- Recent weather conditions.
- Distribution of affected plants.

Do not confirm a disease from visual symptoms alone.
Laboratory testing or agricultural expert inspection
may be needed.

Do not prescribe pesticides or fungicides solely
from AI-generated findings.

Use locally approved agricultural recommendations
and verified disease-management guidance.


WHEAT CROP - DISEASE KNOWLEDGE

1. WHEAT RUST

Common types:
- Yellow rust (stripe rust)
- Brown rust (leaf rust)
- Black rust (stem rust)

Possible symptoms:
- Yellow rust: yellow-orange pustules arranged in stripes on leaves.
- Brown rust: scattered orange-brown pustules on leaf surfaces.
- Black rust: dark brown to black pustules, often on stems and leaves.

Favourable conditions:
Disease development depends on temperature, humidity,
crop variety and environmental conditions.

Monitoring:
Inspect leaves and stems regularly.
Observe the colour, shape and distribution of pustules.
Record crop stage and affected plant areas.

2. POWDERY MILDEW

Possible symptoms:
- White powdery fungal growth on leaf surfaces.
- Yellowing or drying of severely affected leaves.
- Reduced healthy leaf area.

Monitoring:
Inspect upper and lower leaf surfaces.
Record how quickly the symptoms spread.

3. KARNAL BUNT

Possible symptoms:
- Partial replacement of wheat grain contents by dark spores.
- Affected grains may produce a dark appearance and unpleasant odour.

Monitoring:
Inspect harvested grain and seek laboratory confirmation
when Karnal bunt is suspected.

4. LOOSE SMUT

Possible symptoms:
- Grain heads may be replaced by masses of dark spores.
- Infected ears may appear black and powdery.
- Infection may become apparent when ears emerge.

Monitoring:
Inspect emerging wheat ears during crop development.
Record affected plants and consult local agricultural experts.

5. GENERAL DISEASE ADVISORY

Visual symptoms alone cannot confirm a disease.
Nutrient deficiency, water stress and environmental damage
may produce similar symptoms.

Record affected plant parts, symptom distribution,
crop stage and recent weather conditions.

Seek laboratory or agricultural expert confirmation
when disease identification is uncertain.

Do not prescribe fungicides solely from AI-generated findings.
Use locally approved agricultural recommendations.


COTTON CROP - PEST KNOWLEDGE

1. COTTON WHITEFLY

Scientific name:
Bemisia tabaci.

Common identification:
Small white, sap-feeding insects commonly found
on the undersides of cotton leaves.

Possible symptoms:
- Yellowing of leaves.
- Sticky honeydew on leaf surfaces.
- Sooty mould development on honeydew.
- Reduced plant vigour under severe infestation.

Monitoring:
Inspect the undersides of leaves regularly.
Observe adult insects and immature stages.
Record infestation levels and affected field areas.

2. COTTON APHID

Scientific name:
Aphis gossypii.

Common identification:
Small, soft-bodied insects that occur in colonies
on young shoots and leaf undersides.

Possible symptoms:
- Curling and distortion of leaves.
- Sticky honeydew.
- Reduced growth in heavily affected plants.

Monitoring:
Inspect tender shoots and leaf undersides.
Record colony distribution and plant condition.

3. COTTON JASSID

Common identification:
Small, wedge-shaped sap-feeding insects found
on the undersides of cotton leaves.

Possible symptoms:
- Yellowing along leaf margins.
- Leaf curling and downward cupping.
- Browning or drying of leaf edges.
- Severe injury may cause hopperburn.

Monitoring:
Inspect leaf undersides and margins.
Record affected plants and crop growth stage.

4. COTTON BOLLWORMS

Important examples:
- American bollworm (Helicoverpa armigera).
- Pink bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella).

Possible symptoms:
- Feeding holes in flower buds and bolls.
- Damaged flowers and shed fruiting structures.
- Larvae feeding inside developing bolls.
- Internal boll damage.

Monitoring:
Inspect flowers, squares and developing bolls.
Look for larvae, entry holes and feeding damage.
Record infestation and crop stage.

5. GENERAL PEST ADVISORY

Different cotton pests can produce overlapping symptoms.

Inspect insects, affected leaves and damaged bolls
before identifying a suspected pest.

Record crop stage, infestation distribution
and observed damage.

Do not recommend insecticides solely from AI predictions.
Use locally approved agricultural guidance.

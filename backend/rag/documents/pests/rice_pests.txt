
RICE CROP - PEST KNOWLEDGE

1. YELLOW STEM BORER

Common identification:
The yellow stem borer is an insect pest that damages rice
by feeding inside the plant stem.

Possible symptoms:
- Dead heart during vegetative growth.
- White ear or empty panicle during reproductive growth.
- Affected central shoots may dry out.

Monitoring:
Inspect tillers and affected stems.
Look for entry holes and signs of larval activity.
Record crop growth stage and affected plant distribution.

2. BROWN PLANTHOPPER

Common identification:
Brown planthoppers are sap-feeding insects that occur
near the base of rice plants.

Possible symptoms:
- Yellowing and drying of plants in patches.
- Severe infestation may cause hopperburn.
- Insects may be visible near the lower stem.

Monitoring:
Inspect the base of plants regularly.
Observe insect population and crop condition.
Monitor field patches showing unusual drying.

3. LEAF FOLDER

Common identification:
Rice leaf folder larvae fold leaves and feed within them.

Possible symptoms:
- Folded or rolled leaves.
- White or transparent feeding streaks.
- Reduced green leaf area.

Monitoring:
Inspect folded leaves and check for larvae.
Record the percentage of affected leaves.

4. RICE GALL MIDGE

Common identification:
Rice gall midge larvae damage the growing point
and may cause abnormal gall formation.

Possible symptoms:
- Silver shoots or onion-shaped galls.
- Affected tillers may fail to produce normal panicles.

Monitoring:
Inspect tillers for abnormal shoots.
Record affected areas and crop growth stage.

5. GENERAL PEST ADVISORY

Pest symptoms may resemble nutrient deficiencies,
water stress or other crop disorders.

Inspect insects and damaged plant parts before
confirming a pest infestation.

Record crop stage, infestation distribution
and observed damage.

Do not recommend insecticides solely from AI predictions.
Use locally approved agricultural guidance.

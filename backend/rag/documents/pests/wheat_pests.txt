
WHEAT CROP - PEST KNOWLEDGE

1. APHIDS

Common identification:
Aphids are small, soft-bodied insects that may occur in colonies
on wheat leaves, stems and developing ears.

Possible signs:
- Clusters of small insects on plant surfaces.
- Yellowing or curling leaves.
- Sticky honeydew on affected plant surfaces.

Monitoring:
Inspect leaves, stems and ears regularly.
Record infestation levels and crop growth stage.

2. TERMITE

Common identification:
Termites may attack wheat roots and underground plant portions.

Possible signs:
- Drying or dead plants in patches.
- Damaged roots or underground stems.
- Plants that can be pulled out easily.

Monitoring:
Inspect affected patches and examine plant roots.
Record the distribution of damaged plants.

3. ARMYWORM

Common identification:
Armyworm larvae feed on wheat leaves and may cause
significant leaf damage when populations increase.

Possible signs:
- Irregular feeding damage on leaves.
- Missing or heavily damaged leaf portions.
- Visible caterpillars around affected plants.

Monitoring:
Inspect plants and surrounding areas, especially
when feeding damage appears to increase.

4. GENERAL ADVISORY

Do not confirm pest infestation from leaf symptoms alone.
Inspect the actual insects and affected plant parts.

Record crop stage, infestation distribution and severity.
Use locally approved agricultural guidance for management.

Do not recommend insecticides solely from AI-generated findings.
